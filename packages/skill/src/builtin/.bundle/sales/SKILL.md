# Sales

Work sales tasks in this order: gather CRM/account context first, then prepare the specific deliverable, then sanity-check numbers.

## Meeting preparation

- Pull account context before drafting: past meetings, open deals, customer evidence, competitor mentions.
- Draft agenda with stated outcomes per item. Keep the customer's business problem first, product second.
- Include a competitive brief only when the customer is known to be evaluating alternatives.

## Call follow-up

- Recap decisions and action items with owners and dates. Distinguish what the customer committed to from what we committed to.
- Flag follow-ups that will need a decision or budget approval.

## Deal strategy

- State the deal stage, the champion, the economic buyer, and the blocker (if any).
- A deal is not "moving" unless a next step is booked and an owner is assigned. If none exists, name the missing step.

## Forecasts

- Report committed vs pipeline numbers separately. Never blend them.
- When a number is an estimate, mark it as an estimate and state the assumption behind it.

## Evidence hygiene

- Customer evidence (quotes, metrics, references) must be traceable to a real account record. Do not invent testimonials or success metrics.
- If evidence is missing, say so instead of substituting a generic claim.
